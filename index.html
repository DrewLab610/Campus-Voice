How it works

Three steps, no office visit required.

1

Create an account

Your real name, matric number, and email — you're logged in right away.

2

Describe the problem

Pick a category and urgency, write what happened, attach a photo if it helps.

3

Watch it get handled

Admin can approve, start processing, resolve, or reject it — every step is timestamped in your history.
